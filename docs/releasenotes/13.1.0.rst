Other changes
=============

Palette operations after converting transparent images
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Fixed incorrect palette colours and invalid palettes in :py:func:`~PIL.ImageOps.invert`,
:py:func:`~PIL.ImageOps.posterize` and :py:func:`~PIL.ImageOps.solarize` when reusing a
transparent P image with an RGB palette after conversion to RGBA, LA or PA, or saving it as WebP.

Blurring I;16 images
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

:py:class:`~PIL.ImageFilter.GaussianBlur` and :py:class:`~PIL.ImageFilter.BoxBlur`
now support I;16, I;16L, I;16B and I;16N images.
